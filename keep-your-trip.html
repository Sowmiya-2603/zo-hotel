Why guests cancel —
and what we do about it.

We asked every guest who cancelled last year why they did. Five reasons covered nearly all of them, so we redesigned our booking around those five.

42% of cancellations

“My dates stopped working.”

Leave gets moved, a meeting lands on your check-in day, a wedding shifts a week.

Our answer

Move your stay to any available date, as many times as you need, free until 48 hours before check-in. Same room, same rate where available — no rebooking, no fees.

Our answer

THE POINT OF ALL THIS

We'd rather move your stay than lose it.

A cancelled trip helps no one — you lose the holiday, we lose the chance to host you. So every policy on this page is built to give your plans room to breathe instead of forcing a yes-or-no decision weeks in advance.

And if cancelling really is the right call, we make that easy too. The option is always there, in plain sight, with no retention hoops.
