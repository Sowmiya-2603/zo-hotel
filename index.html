BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

The questions families
ask us most.

Short, honest answers — so you know exactly what your stay will be like.

“How close are we to the temple, really?”

A 6-minute walk to the East Gate. For early darshan we arrange drop-offs at the entrance — no parking scramble, no haggling.

“Do the rooms face the gopuram?”

Honestly — no. Our rooms face the quiet garden side. You trade the view for silent nights, and the real thing is six minutes away whenever you want it.

“Will it work for kids and grandparents?”

Family rooms with extra beds, lift access to every floor, Jain and veg meals, and a doctor on call. Grandparents are our most frequent guests.

“We found a lower price elsewhere.”

Compare what's inside the price: breakfast, temple drop-offs, and no checkout surprises. And if the room or dates aren't right, adjusting them takes a minute.
